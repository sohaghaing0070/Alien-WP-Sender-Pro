## 🌟 Overview

**Alien WP Sender Pro** is a high-performance, enterprise-grade desktop automation solution designed for high-throughput WhatsApp marketing, lead generation, and 24/7 intelligent customer engagement. 

Engineered with a **100% self-contained offline architecture**, the software runs immediately on any fresh Windows computer without requiring .NET runtimes, external dependencies, or complex server environments.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Capabilities

### 💬 1. High-Speed Bulk Messaging & Campaign Dispatcher
- **Anti-Ban Protection Engine**: Automated randomized delays, smart warm-up throttling, and message variation.
- **Spintax & Personalization**: Dynamic merge tags (`{Name}`, `{Company}`, `{CustomField}`) with Spintax support (`{Hello|Hi|Greetings}`).
- **Rich Media Broadcasting**: Send text, images, video demonstrations, PDF catalogs, invoices, and documents in bulk.
- **Excel & CSV Ingestion**: Instant drag-and-drop contact import with phone number auto-formatting.

### 🔍 2. Automated Google Maps B2B Lead Extractor
- **Multi-Threaded Real-Time Scraper**: Extract verified business listings, category keywords, addresses, websites, and direct phone numbers across any city, region, or country.
- **One-Click Export**: Export cleansed leads directly to CSV, Excel, or immediately load into active broadcast campaigns.

### 🤖 3. Multi-Modal 24/7 AI Auto-Reply & Call Assistant
- **Universal Modality Recognition**:
  - 💬 **Text Inquiries**: Autonomous product recommendations, pricing quotes, and feature walkthroughs.
  - 🎙️ **Voice Notes & Audio**: Automated audio recognition and instant contextual text assistance.
  - 📷 **Images & Screenshots**: Processes customer screenshots (error screens, receipts, product snapshots) and provides guidance.
  - 📞 **WhatsApp Calls & Missed Calls**: Detects incoming voice/video calls and immediately sends an automated WhatsApp response with product catalogs and live agent contact options.
- **Multi-Lingual Support**: Seamless conversations in Bengali, English, and Banglish.

### 🏢 4. Enterprise Integrations & Companion Modules
- **ZKTeco Biometric Attendance Hub**: Push ADMS cloud integration with automated WhatsApp late/absent notifications.
- **Multi-Store POS & Inventory**: Retail billing, thermal printing, and multi-branch inventory tracking.
- **SMS & OTP Broadcaster**: Transactional OTP gateway supporting GP, Robi, Banglalink, Teletalk, and global Twilio channels.
